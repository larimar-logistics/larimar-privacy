Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

This policy explains how Larimar Logistics (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when a merchant installs the Larimar Decorator app (the “App”) and when a shopper uses the product customizer the App adds to a merchant’s store. It covers the App, the embedded customizer, and the order processing that follows a purchase.

Information we process

Store and order data. When a merchant installs the App and receives orders, we access store details and order information through Shopify’s APIs and webhooks. This includes the store domain, the products managed with the App, and, for each customized order, the order number, the line items and quantities, the customer’s email, the shipping address, and the order totals. We use this to price customizations, to create production-ready files, and to fulfill and produce the order.

Design data from shoppers. When a shopper customizes a product, we process the artwork or images they upload, any text they enter, and the choices they make (placement, colors, sizes, and quantities), along with the resulting design and preview. This is used to render the customizer, to show a live preview, and to produce the finished item.

Payment data. We do not collect or store payment card details. All payments are handled by Shopify and its payment processors; the App never sees card data.

How we use information

  • Render the product customizer and show a live preview of the design.
  • Calculate the price of a customization from the merchant’s configured pricing.
  • Save the design so the order can be produced exactly as approved.
  • Generate production-ready print and embroidery files and a production sheet.
  • Route the order to fulfillment and let authorized staff manage it.
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the service, with providers acting on our instructions:

  • Shopify, the platform the App runs on, as the source of store and order data.
  • Cloud hosting and file storage providers that run the App and store uploaded artwork and production files.
  • Fulfillment and warehouse providers that produce and ship the finished orders.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Data retention

We keep design and order data for as long as needed to fulfill and support the order and to meet legal, accounting, and reporting obligations. We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed, or on a valid request as described below.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Shoppers should contact the store they purchased from; that merchant can reach us to act on the request. Merchants and shoppers can also contact us directly at christopher.rodriguez@larimarlogistics.com.

We honor Shopify’s mandatory data requests. When Shopify sends a customer data request, a customer redaction, or a shop redaction, we respond by providing or deleting the relevant data as required.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including access controls and encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International processing

We and our providers may process information in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Children

The App is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice where appropriate.
